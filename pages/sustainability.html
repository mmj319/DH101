🌱 Sustainability & Computing

Reflecting on the environmental impact, computing costs, and ecological footprint of technology.

Technology, Carbon, & Ecology

The digital cloud is not weightless. Behind every high-parameter generative model, data center server, and training run lies a tangible physical infrastructure consuming vast amounts of fresh water for cooling, electrical power from grids, and rare earth minerals for silicon chips.

⚡ The Hidden Cost of AI Compute

While generating images or text prompts feels instantaneous and ephemeral on our laptops, running billions of matrix multiplications on server farms produces significant carbon emissions and places heavy demands on local electrical grids. Recognizing this physical footprint reminds us to use computational tools mindfully rather than superfluously.

🍃 Lightweight, Static Web Design

This website itself is designed with sustainability and efficiency in mind:

  • Pure Static HTML & CSS: No bloated frameworks, heavy runtime bundles, or background server processing.
  • Optimized Assets: Clean SVG icons, optimized responsive images, and system-level performance.
  • Longevity: Accessible, timeless web standards that load quickly and respect visitors' bandwidth and battery life.

💭 Moving Forward

As digital humanists, part of our responsibility is advocating for green computing policies, renewable-powered data centers, and questioning whether every problem truly requires an energy-intensive AI solution when simpler, human-centered approaches thrive.
