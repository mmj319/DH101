# Week 2 Reflection

Reflect 2: Can AI help us define what it means to be human, or does it reduce us to patterns?

I don’t think AI can help us define what it means to be human. I do think that AI can give us something to compare it to for what it means to be human. But if you sit there and ask the AI that question, I don’t think that is an answer that you should listen to. AI only knows as much about humans as humans know about humans. It would reduce us to patterns, which is how AI thinks. It thinks in terms of patterns, so that's how it would think about humans. AI's explanations of us would not include the nuances or outliers of being human. It would only analyze us in terms of our commonalities. I felt in control for most of what I was doing today for the most part. The only thing slightly pulling from my feeling of control was that I was following a tutorial step by step. But I still got to decide how I approached the tutorial and where I could stop to ask questions.
