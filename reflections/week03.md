# Week 3 Reflection

Reflect 3: What does “authenticity” mean when identity is co-constructed with AI?

When we begin to merge identity in its construction with AI, the notion of authenticity becomes murky. What we are used to hearing as being authentic is remaining true to oneself and not letting other things influence it. But AI is so easily influenced that when it comes into the picture, I believe that this definition comes to change. AI uses multiple different sources for what it gives you, with each influencing that response. Something I'm not sure we can say follows the true definition of authenticity. So, what does it mean to be authentic when your identity is co-constructed with AI? I think that is pretty hard to answer. I guess we could say having minimal influence from the AI but even that seems like a pretty unstable definition. I think the acknowledgment of the co-construction of AI can help play into the authenticity of an identity. Acknowledging that it is no longer just you is very important to that idea.
