Reflection Prompt

"Is AI a collaborator, tool, or plagiarist in storytelling?"

My Reflection

I believe that the question of AI's position in storytelling is a loaded one. Is it a collaborator, tool, or plagiarist in storytelling? I think that it really depends on the way that you are looking at it. When considering how AI is made and how it obtains its data, I believe that AI can be seen as a plagiarist. AI is fed data that exists already in the world. Data that was on the internet. Data that is public-facing. This data was fed to AI to help create its algorithms and to lead to its responses. However, for some of this data, permission was not obtained before feeding it into AI. A story about a mystical land an author worked so hard on and shared to the internet in hopes of recognition gets nabbed and given to AI so that it can use it for reference when someone tells it to "write me a mystical story." These authors do not know that their stories are being used, and they most likely did not give permission for them to be used.

However, if we put this idea aside. I believe that we can see AI as a collaborative tool in writing. It should not be used to write an entire piece, but it can give helpful feedback on what you should write. It should be used more like a peer reviewer rather than an author itself. It can help come up with helpful synonyms or illustrious adjectives. It can tell you when something isn't coming off in a clear way. And in thinking of it as a collaborative tool, you can realize that it has the possibility of being wrong. You're working in collaboration; it is not commanding you to do its bidding. AI can spit out ideas that aren't good for your overall piece, and being aware of that can be incredibly helpful.
