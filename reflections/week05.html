Reflection Prompt

"How does AI alter authorship and remix culture? Who owns AI-made art?"

My Reflection

AI has created a complication in the idea of authorship. The world of writing and being an author has become muddied with the emergence of AI. There has entered more of a distrust between authors as AI has become more popularized, There is a question of if the author wrote something with AI or not and if they did, how much of the work did they have the AI model do for them. If that work threshold is above a certain level, can it really be said that someone wrote something themself, or was it the AI who authored the work and the human who edited it for the bot? We can see this confusion in the AI scanners that many professors have had to start using when looking at the projects their students submit. Many professors would agree that if a work used AI, then it was authored by AI and the student should not get credit. But there are others who disagree with this. They believe AI is a tool that can improve writing. With this sort of debate, a kind of question emerges about who really owns AI art. And I believe the answer is that nobody owns AI art. The user who inputs the prompt may feel that they do because they came up with the idea, but the AI's algorithm has taken art that was made public on the internet and compiled it to give the user what they wanted. So, multiple different artists have contributed to the piece, and it cannot clearly be said who truly owns it.
